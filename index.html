Child Safety Standards

Last Updated: 2026-03-30

1. Our Commitment

At LSV-TECH and Turis, we maintain a zero-tolerance policy against any form of child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, sextortion, trafficking, and any conduct or content that sexually exploits, abuses, or endangers minors.

We strictly prohibit the use of Turis to create, upload, share, distribute, solicit, promote, or facilitate any form of child sexual abuse or exploitation.

2. Prohibited Content and Conduct

  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM), including real, generated, manipulated, or otherwise exploitative visual content involving minors where prohibited by law
  • Grooming or attempting to establish contact with a minor for sexual exploitation
  • Sexual exploitation, sexualization, coercion, or abuse of minors
  • Sextortion involving a minor
  • Trafficking, solicitation, or facilitation of sexual exploitation involving minors
  • Any attempt to use the platform to endanger children
  • Any illegal content or conduct involving minors

3. Reporting and User Safety

Users may report content, accounts, behavior, child safety concerns, app malfunctions, or bugs by contacting:

  • Child Safety Contact: childsafety@lsv-tech.com
  • Support: support@lsv-tech.com
  • Subject line recommendation: Child Safety Report

Turis also provides an in-app reporting channel available in the User Profile section. Through this channel, users may report concerns related to child safety and abuse policies, as well as app malfunctions, technical issues, or bugs.

4. Review and Enforcement

When LSV-TECH obtains actual knowledge of content or conduct that may involve CSAM, CSAE, or child endangerment, we may take appropriate action consistent with our policies and applicable law, including:

  • Immediate content removal or access restriction
  • Account suspension or permanent termination
  • Preservation of relevant records where required by law
  • Escalation for internal review
  • Reporting to law enforcement, the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC), or the relevant regional authority, where applicable

5. Legal Compliance

LSV-TECH is committed to complying with applicable child safety laws and regulations. We maintain procedures intended to support review, enforcement, and legal escalation when child sexual abuse material or exploitative conduct is identified or reported.

6. Child Safety Point of Contact

LSV-TECH designates the following contact for child safety matters and potential notifications related to CSAE or CSAM:

  • Child Safety Point of Contact: childsafety@lsv-tech.com
  • Legal Contact: legal@lsv-tech.com

7. Scope and Application

These Child Safety Standards apply to all users of Turis and to all use of the platform, whether through user accounts, messages, uploaded content, listings, profiles, support interactions, in-app reports submitted through the User Profile section, or any related platform functionality.
